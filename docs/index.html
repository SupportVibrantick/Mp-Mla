MP-MLA Platform Operating Manual

Verified Operating Manual Built Directly from Codebase & Database Schema

1. Introduction & Platform Overview

The MP-MLA Constituency Management SaaS Platform is a web-based management suite built for Members of Parliament (MP), Members of Legislative Assembly (MLA), secretariat teams, and public administration.

Verified System Scope
The platform operates on a multi-tenant PostgreSQL Prisma architecture. Superadmins manage SaaS tenants and subscription paywall controls via the Master Dashboard, while MP/MLA offices manage grievances, projects, funds, Janata Darbar sessions, welfare schemes, voter verification, website creation, and public relations.

System Feature Modules:

  1. Executive Dashboard: Real-time KPI cards, monthly grievance trend charts, priority breakdown, and recent constituent request feeds.
  2. Grievance Management System: Public request intake, SLA countdown timers, department mapping, Kanban board, and proof document resolution.
  3. Janata Darbar Sessions & Tokens: Walk-in digital token engine, VIP priority tagging, session slot reservation, and live hearing minutes.
  4. Events & Protocol Scheduler: Constituency public events, team assignment, protocol guest list RSVP, and speech notes.
  5. Meetings & Appointments: Secretariat meetings scheduler, Minutes of Meeting (MoM) archive, visitor appointment passes.
  6. Voter Portal & Verification: Electoral search by EPIC ID, voter identity verification requests, and demographic analytics (age distribution, gender ratio, ward density).
  7. Geography & Booth Hierarchy: Constituency, District, Block, Town/Village, Polling Location, Booth, and Ward territory mapping.
  8. Infrastructure Projects & Milestones: Work order creation, administrative sanctions, contractor bidding, and 25%/50%/75%/100% stage photo uploads.
  9. Fund Ledger & Financial Audit: MPLAD, MLALAD, State, and Central fund allocations, installment releases, and Utilization Certificates (UC).
  10. Welfare Schemes & Applications: Scheme catalog management, constituent eligibility checks, and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) disbursal tracking.
  11. CRM Contacts & Party Leaders: Stakeholder directory, leader profiling, community groups, and automated birthday/anniversary greeting reminders.
  12. Public Facilities & Institutions: Directory for temples, mosques, hospitals, schools, police stations, CSC centers, and parks.
  13. Website Builder Studio: Visual page builder, landing page creator, custom domain routing, SSL management, and live deployment.
  14. Creative Studio & Social Media: Banner generator, festival wish posters, social post analytics, and press releases.
  15. Competitor Analysis: Opposition leader metrics, local issue sentiment tracking, and competitor activity logs.
  16. Helplines, Documents & Reports: Admin emergency helpline numbers, central file vault, and PDF/Excel report export engine.
  17. Master Dashboard & Subscriptions: Tenant onboarding, subscription plans (Free 14-day Trial, Pro, Enterprise), paywall enforcement, audit logs, and soft-delete Recycle Bin.

4. Master Superadmin & Multi-Tenant Setup

Superadmins manage all SaaS constituency accounts from the Master Dashboard (`/master-dashboard`).

1

Access Master Dashboard

Log in as Superadmin at /master-dashboard/login. Open the Tenants Directory page.

2

Provision New Constituency Account

Click Add Tenant. Enter Representative Name (e.g., Hon. Ashok Kumar MP), Constituency Type (Lok Sabha / Vidhan Sabha), State, District, and Subdomain.

3

Assign Subscription Plan & Initial Credentials

Select plan tier (14-Day Free Trial, Pro Plan, Enterprise Plan) and set initial Admin login credentials.

5. Subscriptions & Paywall Enforcement Rules

The automated billing system handles free trial countdowns, plan renewals, and paywall locks.

Subscription Paywall Lock System
If a 14-day free trial expires without plan renewal, the platform activates a Paywall Lock Banner. Staff can still view records, but write operations (creating new grievances, voter roll edits, project sanctioning) are locked until plan renewal at /account/billing.

6. System Audit Logs & Data Recovery (Recycle Bin)

All system actions, logins, updates, and deletions are recorded in immutable audit logs.

Two-Stage Soft Delete & Recovery
Deleted items (grievances, contacts, voter entries, projects) are retained in the Recycle Bin for 30 days before permanent deletion, allowing instant restoration.

7. Executive Dashboard Operating Guide

The Executive Dashboard serves as the central command post for the MP or MLA.

  • KPI Summary Metrics: Total Grievances, Sanctioned Funds, Active Projects, Voter Satisfaction Score.
  • Monthly Trends & Priority Breakdown: Visual charts categorizing complaints by priority (Critical, High, Medium, Low) and status.
  • Quick Action Command Bar: Instant shortcuts for Log Grievance, Sanction Project, Voter Search, and Janata Darbar.

8. Grievances & Public Requests Operating Guide

Constituent complaints follow an SLA-monitored lifecycle to guarantee timely resolution:

1

Complaint Intake & Department Assignment

Navigate to /grievances or /public-requests/new. Enter Constituent Name, Phone, Ward/Booth, Department (PWD, Electricity, Water Board, Health), and Priority.

2

Department Escalation & SLA Countdown

The ticket is assigned to the Nodal Department Officer with an automated SLA timer (e.g. 48 hours for emergency repairs).

3

Action Taken & Resolution Sign-Off

Department officers upload proof photos and action notes. Upon confirmation, ticket status changes to Resolved.

9. Janata Darbar Sessions Operating Guide

Streamline walk-in hearings at /janata-darbar:

  • Digital Token Engine: Issue sequential tokens (e.g. JD-042) for walk-in constituents with time slots.
  • Priority Tagging: Flag senior citizens, medical urgent cases, or official delegations for priority queue.
  • Live Hearing Minutes: Record MP/MLA verbal instructions directly against the constituent's profile during the hearing.

10. Events & Protocol Scheduler Guide

Manage constituency public functions, rallies, and official protocol events at /events:

  • Event Scheduling: Set Date, Time, Venue, Event Category (Public Rally, Inauguration, Inspection), and Budget.
  • Protocol Guest List & RSVP: Manage VIP guest lists, send official invitations, and track RSVP responses.
  • Speech & Briefing Notes: Attach official speech notes, talking points, and constituency stats for the MP/MLA.

11. Meetings & Appointments Operating Guide

Organize secretariat meetings and constituent appointments at /meetings and /appointments:

  • Official Meetings & Minutes (MoM): Schedule departmental review meetings, record attendees, and archive Minutes of Meeting (MoM).
  • VIP & Visitor Appointments: Schedule visitor slots, issue digital entry passes, and maintain visitor logs.

12. Voter Portal & Identity Verification Operating Guide

The **Voter Portal** (`/voter-portal`, `/voter-verification`, `/voter-list`) provides complete electoral roll search and verification.

Voter Portal Operating Workflow:

1

Electoral Search by EPIC Card or Name

Go to /voter-portal. Enter EPIC Card Number (e.g. ABC1234567) or constituent name to fetch booth number, serial number, and polling station location.

2

Voter Identity Verification

Review new voter registration or address correction requests. Mark status as Verified, Pending Verification, or Needs Revision.

3

Demographic Analytics

Analyze Age Distribution charts (18-25, 26-35, 36-50, 50+), Gender Ratio, and Ward-wise Voter Density heatmaps.

13. Demographics, Wards & Booths Hierarchy Guide

Manage constituency territorial structure at /geography:

Constituency ➔ District ➔ Block ➔ Town/Village ➔ Polling Location ➔ Booth / Ward

  • Bulk Import: Upload CSV/Excel files containing electoral roll booth lists via /geography/bulk-import.
  • Polling Location Audit: Record electricity, water, ramps, and security facilities available at each booth.

14. Public Facilities & Institutions Guide

Manage public infrastructure and community assets at /public-facilities:

  • Institution Categories: Map Temples, Mosques, Hospitals, Schools, Colleges, Police Stations, CSC Centers, Parks, and Community Halls.
  • Facility Directory Requests: Review registration requests for local public facilities via /register-public-facility.

15. Infrastructure Projects Operating Guide

Monitor constituency development works at /projects:

1

Create Project Entry

Enter Project Title, Category (Road, Community Hall, School, Solar Light), Estimated Budget, Ward, and Contractor Name.

2

Stage-wise Construction Milestone Verification

Upload site progress photos at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% completion milestones before approving payment releases.

16. Funds & Financial Ledger Audit Guide

Track annual fund allocations and expenditure audit ledgers at /funds and /funds/overview:

  • Fund Categories: Manage MPLAD, MLALAD, State Fund, Central Fund, and CSR allocations.
  • Installment Releases & UC Vault: Track installment releases from District Collectorate and archive Utilization Certificates (UC).
  • Head-wise Balance Ledger: View real-time balance remaining across education, healthcare, roads, and drinking water heads.

17. Welfare Schemes & Applications Guide

Manage state and central welfare schemes at /schemes and /schemes/applications:

  • Scheme Repository: Define scheme guidelines, eligibility criteria (Age, Income, Gender, Social Category), and required documents.
  • Automated Eligibility Engine: Match constituent profiles with active schemes automatically.
  • Application Disbursal Tracking: Process applications, review uploaded documents, and track Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) status.

18. CRM Contacts & Party Leaders Guide

Manage constituency stakeholders and party organization at /crm/contacts and /leaders:

  • Constituent CRM Contacts: Maintain detailed profile history, address, voting ward, and past request logs.
  • Party Leaders Directory: Categorize party workers, booth presidents, youth wing leaders, and local influencers.
  • Birthday & Anniversary Reminders: View upcoming birthdays at /leaders/birthdays and dispatch automated greeting messages.

19. Website Builder Studio & Domains Guide

Create and host official constituency web portals at /website/builder:

1

Build Web Pages

Use the drag-and-drop page builder to assemble homepages, news portals, photo galleries, and request forms.

2

Map Custom Subdomains

Configure web domains (e.g. mp-ashokkumar.in) with SSL security certificates at /website/domains.

3

Live Deployment

Publish website updates instantly with live deployment status logs at /website/deployments.

20. Creative Studio & Social Media Guide

Manage public relations graphics and social outreach at /creatives and /social:

  • Creative Studio Poster Generator: Overlay MP/MLA photographs, party emblems, and festival wish text onto high-res graphic templates.
  • Social Media Analytics: Track post engagement, constituent reach, and follower metrics.
  • Press Release Storage: Archive official media statements, event photos, and press briefings.

21. Competitors Analysis Operating Guide

Track opposition political activities and sentiment at /competitors:

  • Opposition Profiling: Maintain records of rival candidates, their strongholds, and political statements.
  • Sentiment & Activity Monitor: Track local political trends, campaign promises, and public sentiment.

22. Document Vault & PDF Reports Guide

Manage central documents and analytics reports at /documents and /reports/pdf:

  • Central Document Vault: Securely upload official sanction letters, government circulars, and parliamentary questions.
  • Executive PDF Summary Reports: Generate formal monthly activity reports for the MP/MLA office.
  • Excel Data Export Engine: Export filtered grievance, voter, project, and scheme datasets to Excel format.

23. Frequently Asked Questions & Support

Q1: How do I search for a voter in the Voter Portal?

Navigate to /voter-portal from the sidebar. Enter the EPIC Card number or constituent name in the search bar.

Q2: Can I export project sanction details to PDF?

Yes. Go to /reports/pdf or open /projects and click Export PDF Report to download formal summaries.

Q3: What happens if a free trial expires?

The system activates a subscription paywall banner. Read access remains active, while write access is locked until plan renewal at /account/billing.
